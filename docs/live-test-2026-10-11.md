## Fixes (merged)

- #78 c2a992a: EKS grants the deploying user cluster admin. New clusters answered Unauthorized to the user who made them.
- #79 c43b569: OpenShift host gets crc from mirror.openshift.com (the old URL 404s) and puts it on sudo's PATH.
- #80 db7e672:
  - Helm add-ons own their namespace, so take it down removes it.
  - Karpenter may create the Spot service-linked role.
  - The HPA demo runs without the demo app (`hpa=off`), and the failure has a hint.
- #81 ef13409 (Gabriel): CONNECT reads `--role` as AWS CLI v2 writes it.
- #82 24ada30: the metrics-server add-on lets the viewer group read nodes and metrics. On a fresh cluster CONNECT gave a viewer that could not list nodes.
- #83 917be84: the ALB availability SLI counts the ALB's own 503s, which RequestCount leaves out (found in the drill).
- #84 82bc119: DISCONNECT also removes the cluster entry that CONNECT wrote. Without it, ~/.kube/config kept `arn:…:cluster/cs-drill` after DISCONNECT.

## Findings not fixed

- **Karpenter Spot** fails with `AuthFailure.ServiceLinkedRoleCreationNotPermitted` even with #80. `simulate-principal-policy` says allowed, so this is likely an SCP. Creating `AWSServiceRoleForEC2Spot` once by hand would fix it, but that is an account-wide change I left to Gabriel. Karpenter also logged `pricing:GetProducts` AccessDenied, which is not critical.
- **Learn lab checks** run kubectl against the current kube context. On Gabriel's machine that is `ppf-helm`, his work cluster. The check should pin a context. **Fixed in #96** (d1d25b1): a check needs `CLOUDSPIDER_LAB_CONTEXT`, refuses `ppf-helm`/`ppf-*` and non-lab names, and pins every kubectl/oc to that context.
- **Live stack card.** A healthy live stack shows a red "the stack still has N resources…" line on the card and in the radio. Tests assert this; a calmer color is suggested.
- **Card display.** The card NAME field shows the default name, not the configured one. `elapsedMs` is off early in a run.
- **Add-on tree on `cloudspider-up`.** It shows pod-identity and metrics-server as "absent" although they exist as EKS add-ons, installed by the demo stack.
- **Map after teardown.** The map lagged about 3 minutes, showing "4 running" before it refreshed to "1 running". CloudTrail errors during EKS deletion (InvalidPermission.NotFound) show as red radio noise.
- **Tagging API.** It lists stale ARNs after teardown. Every listed ENI and volume was checked: NotFound. The instances are terminated.
- **CRDs.** Helm uninstall leaves the CRDs (kyverno, cert-manager, keda, karpenter).

**Stuck items.**
- **PVC volume.** `vol-071d34e8c8e0238a8` (cs-drill-dynamic-pvc…, tags `kubernetes.io/cluster/cs-drill=owned`) outlived the cluster. pgcluster's take it down returns once the PVC is deleted. The EBS CSI controller deletes the volume asynchronously, and here the add-ons and cluster went down before it did. The likely trigger is the drill's node loss: the volume had just been force-detached from a dead node. Suggestion: pgcluster's destroy should wait until its PVs are gone, or EKS take it down should list volumes tagged `kubernetes.io/cluster/<name>=owned` and offer to delete them. **Fixed in #95** (10e9d15): pgcluster's take it down waits up to 10 minutes for the stack's CSI-made volumes to go and deletes any left `available` (only volumes with the stack's tags and the EBS CSI tag).
- Nothing else was stuck: no ENIs, LBs, target groups, SGs, finalizers, log groups or IAM roles.

**Final sweep at 14:08.** The only difference from the morning baseline (`baseline-aws.txt`) was the orphan volume, and it is now NotFound.
